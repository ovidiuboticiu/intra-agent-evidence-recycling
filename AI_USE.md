# AI Use and Contribution Disclosure

The Intra-Agent Evidence Recycling (IAER) research program is **human-led and AI-assisted**.

The original research topic, the initial scientific question, and the decision to pursue this line of investigation were proposed by **Ovidiu Boticiu**. During the subsequent development of the project, ChatGPT (OpenAI) was used as an intellectual and technical assistance tool.

AI assistance included the synthesis and structuring of methodological alternatives, discussion and critique of experimental designs, assistance with protocol development, coding and code review, statistical and logical checks, documentation, manuscript drafting and editing, and methodological audit support.

The use of ChatGPT did not constitute autonomous scientific decision-making. Alternative approaches and interpretations suggested through AI-assisted discussion were reviewed by the human researcher, who selected, rejected, or modified them as appropriate.

Ovidiu Boticiu retained responsibility for the final methodological decisions, authorization and execution of experimental runs, supervision of data collection, interpretation of results, decisions to continue, modify, stop, or publish experimental stages, and approval of all public research materials.

The experimental observations and numerical results reported by IAER derive from the executed experimental protocols and model outputs, not from ChatGPT-generated claims or simulated data.

ChatGPT is not listed as an author and is not treated as an independent scientific authority or evidentiary source. Its role was that of an AI-assisted methodological, analytical, technical, and writing tool.

All scientific claims, interpretations, experimental decisions, and published materials were reviewed and approved by the human author, who retains full responsibility for the integrity, accuracy, limitations, and conclusions of the work.
