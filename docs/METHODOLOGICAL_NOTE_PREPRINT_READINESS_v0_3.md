PASS.

The manuscript has one central methodological thesis:

> Before interpreting an absent cross-family LLM effect as a non-replication, the candidate configuration should first demonstrate the prerequisites required to interpret the transferred measurement instrument.

- conceptual priority for repeated/dependent-evidence effects;
- a general IAER mechanism;
- IAER replication on Phi or Ministral;
- IAER non-replication on Phi or Ministral;

- universal validation of the proposed reporting taxonomy.

The two records should cross-reference one another, but neither should replace the other.
