# POWER / SAMPLE SIZE NOTE — v0.4.3

N=32 paired items is retained from the confirmatory v0.4 family.

The minimum effect of interest is an absolute paired risk difference of 25 percentage
points for each co-primary contrast.

This is a confirmatory test of medium-to-large effects suggested by the calibration
pilot, not a high-powered test for arbitrarily small effects. A failed hypothesis means
the preregistered medium-to-large effect was not confirmed under this design.
