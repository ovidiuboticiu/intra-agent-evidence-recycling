# POWER / SAMPLE SIZE NOTE — v0.4.2

N=32 paired items is retained from the frozen v0.4 family.
The minimum effect of interest is an absolute paired risk difference of 25 points
for each co-primary contrast.

This design is intended to confirm medium-to-large effects suggested by v0.3.1,
not arbitrarily small effects. A null result should therefore be interpreted
against this minimum effect of interest rather than as proof of zero effect.
