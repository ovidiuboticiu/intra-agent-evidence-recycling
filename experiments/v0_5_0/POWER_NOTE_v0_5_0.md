# POWER NOTE — v0.5.0

The fixed sample size is 32 paired confirmatory items, matching v0.4.3.

The dual support rule requires both a minimum paired risk difference of +0.25
and a two-sided exact McNemar p-value below 0.05. With 32 pairs, an observed
8-versus-0 discordance yields RD=0.25 and exact p=0.0078125, satisfying both
criteria. Designs with adverse discordance require a larger favorable count.

The study is intended as a direct fixed-N replication, not an effect-size
estimation study powered for small effects. Failure to meet both thresholds is
reported as NOT SUPPORTED, provided all validity gates pass.
