# POWER / SAMPLE-SIZE NOTE

N=32 paired items is a compute-constrained confirmatory design aimed at medium-to-large
effects, not subtle effects.

For orientation, under an illustrative paired-discordance model with:
- 55% of pairs discordant in the predicted direction,
- 5% discordant in the opposite direction,
- the remainder concordant,

an exact two-sided McNemar test at alpha=0.025 has approximately 96% power at N=32.

If the true effect is substantially smaller or has many opposite-direction discordances,
power falls materially. Therefore a non-significant v0.4 should not be interpreted as
proof that arbitrarily small effects do not exist.

The preregistered minimum effect of interest is an absolute paired risk difference of
25 percentage points for each co-primary contrast.
